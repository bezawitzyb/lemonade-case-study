Go / No-Go Decision & Risk-Scoring Framework

Safe to launch

  • Documented workaround covers 100% of cases
  • No compliance or regulatory exposure
  • No customer-facing impact
  • Ops absorbs the overhead at production scale

Requires mitigation

  • Workaround possible but adds manual load
  • Monitoring / escalation path confirmed
  • Customer impact quantified and contained
  • Eng commits to fix sprints

Blocks launch

  • No workaround possible — customer-harm pathway open
  • Regulatory or compliance breach possible
  • Customer impact not quantified / contained
  • SLA failure risk is unacceptable at production scale
Composite risk = (Customer Harm × 0.35) + (Regulatory Exposure × 0.40) + (Scale-at-Peak × 0.25), each dimension scored 1–5. Regulatory exposure carries the heaviest weight because protecting the UK licence is non-negotiable.  Band: <2.0 Safe · 2.0–4 Mitigate · ≥4 Block.
Decision rule: two or more issues in the Block band → No-Go; a single Block or any Mitigate → Conditional Go (contained with guardrails); all Safe → Full Go.
