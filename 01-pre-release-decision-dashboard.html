Go / No-Go Decision & Risk-Scoring Framework

Safe to launch

  • Workaround covers 100% of cases
  • No compliance / regulatory exposure
  • No customer-facing impact
  • Ops absorbs overhead at production scale

Requires mitigation

  • Workaround exists but adds manual load
  • Customer impact quantified & contained
  • Fix committed within 2–4 weeks
  • Monitoring / escalation path confirmed

Block feature

  • No workaround — customer-harm pathway open
  • Regulatory / compliance breach possible
  • Cannot scale under real production volume
  • SLA failure risk unacceptable at peak
Composite risk = (Customer Harm × 0.35) + (Regulatory Exposure × 0.40) + (Scale-at-Peak × 0.25), each dimension scored 1–5. Regulatory exposure carries the heaviest weight because protecting the UK licence is non-negotiable.  Band: <2.0 Safe · 2.0–3.5 Mitigate · ≥3.5 Block — modified by workaround coverage (a high score with a manual override + monitoring becomes Mitigate + Volume Cap, while a high score with no workaround and an open harm pathway is a hard Block).
